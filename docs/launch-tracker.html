    Decide in week 1

    Turn on Stripe Tax?

    Reason to say yesSome US states tax digital subscriptions. Stripe Tax figures it out and files the numbers for 0.5% of each sale.
    Reason to say noUnder most states' thresholds you owe nothing yet, and 0.5% adds up once sales grow.
    My pick for youLeave it off for launch. Turn it on before you pass about $5,000 in sales or 100 paying customers, after a 30-minute chat with an accountant.

    Decide in month 1

    Pay a lawyer to review Privacy and Terms?

    Reason to say yesBrands will sign contracts based on these. A review costs a few hundred dollars and protects you if a deal goes wrong.
    Reason to say noThe pages already cover v2's data flows plainly. With no brand revenue yet, the risk is small.
    My pick for youWait until your first brand is ready to pay, then get a one-off review before you send the invoice.
